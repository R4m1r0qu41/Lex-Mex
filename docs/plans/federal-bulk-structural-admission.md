## Admission rules

Verify candidate identity uniqueness, acquisition/source digests and retained
manifest matching before writing. Never overwrite an existing instrument or
adapter. Keep the new candidates' structural baselines unfrozen: passing parser
counts are observations, not independent human verification. Their
`counts_not_frozen` warnings remain visible. LAEM/LAMNI inspected baselines stay
frozen. Preserve decree ownership and publication-range metadata already present
in the repaired adapters. An unexpected parse/body/provenance difference or
blocking validation error stops only that candidate, retaining its evidence and
recording an exception. Unknown temporal states do not block admission.
