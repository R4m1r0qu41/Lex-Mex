# LGPP and LGIPE: SCJN invalidity notes, article by article (2026-11-13)

Status classification for JRH. Every non-legal question in the first draft was resolved from
the retained text (see "Resolved from the text"). No status is changed by this table. Source: the
printed notes in the committed provision text, from the retained Cámara PDFs. Ruling: AI 71/2023 and
joined cases, notified 23-06-2023, published DOF 24-11-2023 (the same ruling as `lgmime`).

Kinds: `suprimido` = the unit was added by the invalid decree and is removed; `recupera` = the text
as of 2 March 2023 regains force (this includes an invalidated *derogación* or *recorrido*, which
brings the article back). Proposed status: `partially_repealed` when some sub-unit is `suprimido`,
otherwise `effective`. Whole articles added by the decree and suppressed ("[Artículo 80 Bis.
Suprimido]") are listed separately: they are repealed, but the parser has folded them into the
preceding article (a parser defect, fixed separately; non-legal).

## Resolved from the text

- LGIPE art. 80's four article-level notes belong to arts. 80 Bis to 80 Quinquies, which the decree
  added and the ruling suppressed. Art. 80 itself is in force. 20 such folded articles exist, all in
  LGIPE; none elsewhere in the corpus.
- The 2 LGPP and 10 LGIPE provisions missing from the first draft print "Derogación del artículo
  declarada inválida y recupera su vigencia" (11) or "Recorrido del inciso declarado inválido" (1):
  the 2023 repeal or renumbering was invalidated, so the prior text is in force.
- LGIPE's `unknown` statuses are not caused by the ruling: 561 of 567 LGIPE provisions have never
  been temporally analyzed. LGPP: 9 of 106.
