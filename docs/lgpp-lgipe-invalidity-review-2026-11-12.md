# LGPP and LGIPE: SCJN invalidity notes, article by article (2026-11-12)

Prepared for JRH review. No status is changed by this table. Source: the printed notes in the
committed provision text (`corpus/mx/<slug>/provisions.json`, from the retained Cámara PDFs).
Ruling: AI 71/2023 and joined cases, notified 23-06-2023, published DOF 24-11-2023 (same as `lgmime`).

Legend. *Scope*: the units the notes name. *Kind*: `suprimido` = the unit is removed (no prior
text to restore); `recupera` = the prior text (as of 2 March 2023) regains force; `inválido` = invalid,
no restoration wording. *Proposed*: `repealed` only when the whole article is `suprimido`;
`partially_repealed` when some unit is `suprimido`; otherwise `effective` (restored text in force).

The LGIPE `unknown` statuses are not caused by the ruling: 561 of 567 LGIPE provisions have never
been temporally analyzed (basis empty). LGPP: 9 of 106 unanalyzed.

Caveats for the reviewer:
- The table is generated by a script that matches the printed form "<Unidad> declarado(a)
  inválido(a) … DOF 24-11-2023". A looser search ("nválid" anywhere) finds 53 LGPP and 240 LGIPE
  provisions; the difference is text that mentions invalidity in another sense or notes in a
  different form. Those are not in the table yet.
- A `repealed` proposal can be wrong when an article holds several article-level notes (for
  example LGIPE art. 80 with four "Artículo … suprimido" notes). Check each against the PDF.
- The notes stay in the text as printed, the same as last-reform notes. Nothing is added.
