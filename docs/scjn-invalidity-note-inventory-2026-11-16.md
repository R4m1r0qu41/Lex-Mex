# SCJN invalidity notes in the corpus: inventory (2026-11-16)

Read-only measurement on branch `status/in-force-scjn` (base `47a3ffc59`), for the [status pass plan](plans/corpus-in-force-status-pass.md). Nothing in the corpus was changed. This is draft 1. The note grammar is still being closed, so these counts are a working inventory, not the final list.

## Totals

- Provisions whose text contains `invalid…` or `inconstitucional`, accents ignored: **714**.
- Provisions with at least one recognised note of the form "<unit> declarado [parcialmente] inválido / inconstitucional": **457**, in **39** instruments.
- Notes by kind: recupera 638, plain 250, suprimido 104, parcial 7.
  - **recupera:** the note says "recupera su vigencia".
  - **suprimido:** a unit added by the decree is removed.
  - **parcial:** the note says "declarado parcialmente inválido".
  - **plain:** invalid, with no revival stated.
- Units the notes name: inciso 261, numeral 260, articulo 152, parrafo 131, fraccion 110, derogacion 71, recorrido 6, subinciso 4, capitulo 2, fracciones 1, apartado 1.
- **Residue that looks like an SCJN note but is not recognised: 55 provisions**, listed below. None is classified automatically.
- **Residue that is the law's own wording** ("pensión de invalidez", "invalida el contrato"), with no mention of the SCJN, an acción or a declaratoria: 202 provisions. The most frequent are `lss` 56, `lissste` 40, `reg-pmimss` 14, `lamp` 8, `reg-lss-macerf` 6, `lft` 5, `lisr` 4, `lrfiyii-art105` 4.

## Classification from context (supersedes the residue counts above)

A Cámara note has a fixed structure: "<unit> declarado [parcialmente] inválido / inconstitucional
… por sentencia / resolutivos / Declaratoria … de la SCJN". The law's own wording about
invalidity never has that "declarado … por sentencia" form. LOPJF, the Amparo law and CPEUM
art. 105 describe how invalidity works; LSS and LISSSTE speak of "pensión de invalidez". Read
this way, all 714 provisions fall into two groups:

- **Real notes: 460 provisions.**
  - 457 have the full form.
  - 3 are short continuations of the note before them: "Declarado parcialmente inválido por
    sentencia …" in `lfsv` 77, and "Declarado inválido por sentencia … (En la porción normativa
    …)" in `lgs` 235 and 247. They take the unit of that earlier note: the article in `lfsv` 77,
    the paragraph in `lgs` 235 and 247.
- **The law's own wording: 254 provisions.** None contains the note form anywhere, so none is an
  invalidation. This includes the 55 "SCJN-looking" items listed below.
- **Ambiguous: none, on this one pattern.** The second, independent test is the Cámara lists
  of rulings, below.

**Reverse search against the Cámara lists** (retrieved 2026-11-16: Acciones de
Inconstitucionalidad `inconst_1.htm` and `inconst.htm`, Controversias Constitucionales
`controv.htm`, Declaratorias Generales `declara.htm`). Of 1,013 note occurrences:
- **911** match a list entry by printed DOF date and ruling kind;
- **92** print only a notification date, such as "Resolutivos … notificados … el 08-05-2023" in
  LGRA and LGCS. They are matched through the ruling;
- **5** match a list date under another kind;
- **5** were not found on the lists: LDSCA (DOF 07-12-2007, 3) and LOPDC (DOF 16-05-2008, 2).
  The row parse is not yet exact, so this is not confirmed.

**Limits of this check:**
- A date-and-kind match shows consistency, not identity, because some dates carry several
  rulings.
- Only about 43 of roughly 1,600 rulings link the law they affect. So the list-to-corpus
  direction ("a law absent from the lists has no invalidation") can be run only for those.

**Known error in the kind counts.** A revival stated in a later sentence of the same note was
counted as `plain`. For example, LGCS: "Artículo declarado inválido … Artículo que 'recupera su
vigencia con el texto que tenía al 27 de diciembre de 2022' …". So `plain` is overcounted.
The final classifier reads every note sentence of a unit.

they are clearly not invalid".
